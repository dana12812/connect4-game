How to Play

  1. Click a column to drop your piece.
  2. Connect 4 in a row — across, up-down, or diagonally — to win.
  3. Pause stops the clock.
  4. New Game clears the board, keeps score and names.
  5. Reset Game wipes everything back to a fresh start.
